# Databricks Data Analyst Associate

Study notes organized by the official exam sections (the **October 2025
guide** — 9 sections, with AI/BI Dashboards and Genie spaces as first-class
topics), one folder per section, ordered by section number. The weight next
to each section is its share of the exam — spend your study time accordingly.
45 scored questions, 90 minutes, 70% to pass.

| # | Section | Weight |
|---|---------|--------|
| 01 | [Understanding the Data Intelligence Platform](01-data-intelligence-platform/) | 11% |
| 02 | [Managing Data](02-managing-data/) | 8% |
| 03 | [Importing Data](03-importing-data/) | 5% |
| 04 | [Executing Queries with Databricks SQL and SQL Warehouses](04-executing-queries/) | 20% |
| 05 | [Analyzing Queries](05-analyzing-queries/) | 15% |
| 06 | [Working with Dashboards and Visualizations](06-dashboards-and-visualizations/) | 16% |
| 07 | [Developing, Sharing, and Maintaining AI/BI Genie Spaces](07-genie-spaces/) | 12% |
| 08 | [Data Modeling with Databricks SQL](08-data-modeling/) | 5% |
| 09 | [Securing Data](09-securing-data/) | 8% |

Sections 04 + 05 are **35% of the exam** — writing SQL and understanding why
it ran the way it did. Add dashboards (16%) and Genie (12%) and you have
nearly two-thirds; the platform/governance sections mostly reuse knowledge
from the engineering tracks one level lighter.

## Relationship to the engineering tracks

This exam looks at the same platform from the **consumption side**: SQL
warehouses instead of clusters, Catalog Explorer instead of DDL, dashboards
instead of pipelines. Where a topic goes deeper on the engineering side, each
section README links to
[`data-engineer-associate/`](../data-engineer-associate/) or
[`data-engineer-professional/`](../data-engineer-professional/).
