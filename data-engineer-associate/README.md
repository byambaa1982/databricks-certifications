# Databricks Data Engineer Associate

✅ **Earned** — [credential](https://credentials.databricks.com/056070f5-1581-4e24-8996-82b1f1e23b20#acc.3C6pjHkb)

Study notes organized by the official exam sections (the **May 2026 guide** —
the exam moved from 5 sections to 7), one folder per section, ordered by
section number. The weight next to each section is its share of the exam —
spend your study time accordingly. 45 scored questions, 90 minutes.

| # | Section | Weight |
|---|---------|--------|
| 01 | [Databricks Intelligence Platform](01-databricks-intelligence-platform/) | 6% |
| 02 | [Data Ingestion and Loading](02-data-ingestion-and-loading/) | 21% |
| 03 | [Data Transformation and Modeling](03-data-transformation-and-modeling/) | 22% |
| 04 | [Working with Lakeflow Jobs](04-lakeflow-jobs/) | 16% |
| 05 | [Implementing CI/CD](05-implementing-cicd/) | 10% |
| 06 | [Troubleshooting, Monitoring & Optimization](06-troubleshooting-monitoring-and-optimisation/) | 10% |
| 07 | [Governance and Security](07-governance-and-security/) | 15% |

Sections 02 + 03 are **43% of the exam** — ingestion and transformation are
the game. Master those, then Lakeflow Jobs (16%) and Governance (15%), and
the remaining quarter follows.

## Relationship to the Professional folder

The Associate covers much of the same ground as
[`data-engineer-professional/`](../data-engineer-professional/), one level
shallower and with more emphasis on *operating the platform* than on edge-case
semantics. The notebooks here are self-contained, but each section README
points to the Professional folder for the deep-dive version of overlapping
topics — study Associate → Professional and the second pass is mostly review.
